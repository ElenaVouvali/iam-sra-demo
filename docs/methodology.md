A sparse-answer sufficiency review can mark unsupported initial candidate topics as needing clarification; they are not pursued as citizen-named issues or numerically scored. A later citizen topic clarification replaces such an unconfirmed candidate without inheriting its inferred stance/conditions; the original proposal text and candidate history remain retained. The metadata call is a fallible semantic judgment, not a lexical veto or confidence estimate. Generic approval/rejection must not create knowledge or institutional mistrust. A bounded factual-evidence review checks model-proposed demonstrated-awareness claims before confirmation. Scope review checks position and conditional willingness as well as topic eligibility. Contrary original statements create an explicit clarification requirement, not silent recency precedence.

Personal privacy, data security, perceived/technical physical safety, medical benefit and distributive equity remain distinct canonical facets. Residential rerouting alone does not create visual/infrastructure scores. Outside-registry wording is retained with unmapped status/issue ID; it has no numeric item. ## Construct and evidence mapping

The current construct is **Provisional FN-aligned scenario readiness**: concern-specific position, expressed conditions and willingness under those conditions. Original-route stance remains separate, globally and per concern. Acceptance of medical/public benefits, awareness, original-route acceptance, conditions and hypothetical modifications are different records. A concerned citizen willing to accept a changed route is not automatically a categorical refuser, nor automatically highly ready. A low privacy/noise score can coexist with high medical-public-benefit support.

Every registry entry includes scope, inclusion/exclusion criteria, facets and illustrative anchors in `configs/concerns.json`. The general 1–9 anchors in `configs/scoring.json` distinguish refusal even under modifications (1), strong boundaries (2), strong disruption with conditional openness (3), conditional local intrusion (4), mixed readiness (5), tentative acceptance (6), qualified acceptance (7), clear support (8) and unqualified concern-specific endorsement (9). The single FN example cannot calibrate all nine levels or all 15 topics. These distinctions are engineering choices requiring FN review.

For FN alignment, lifesaving hospital/public-service support maps to the **medical_public_benefit** facet within welfare_equity. Distributive equity is a distinct facet: no equitable access, fair distribution or equity knowledge is inferred from medical support. `medical_public_benefit_support` is also displayed as an interpretation, but it is never another numeric item in the aggregate. Each canonical concern counts once even with two facets.

Personal surveillance discomfort maps to perceived_safety_privacy; system failure mechanisms, hacking, data retention or access governance require separate technical evidence. Seeing cameras watch property does not alone establish visual pollution. The FN attribution of low altitude/rerouting to visual pollution is ambiguous. We permit a broader **visible_physical_intrusion** facet only if the answer separately expresses an objection to visible aircraft; a mapping note and supporting passage are required. Low altitude alone, privacy camera visibility or a rerouting condition alone is insufficient. Strict mapping therefore may omit FN’s visual score4 rather than invent sky-clutter statements.

Residential rerouting is an acceptance condition unless physical facility siting, construction, zoning or allocation of land is separately expressed. It is not automatically infrastructure/land use, SUMP integration, accessibility or airspace capacity. Shared passages may support independent acoustic/privacy meanings, with an explicit `distinct_shared_evidence` decision and rationale; one objection must not be multiplied across concerns. Additional genuinely supported topics are permitted and are visible in mean inputs, so their effect is reviewable.

Missing/ambiguous evidence is unassessed/null, never zero or a neutral5. Ambiguous mappings require clarification and retain their reasons. There is no inference from verbosity, civic-minded language, opposition, psychological motives or hidden technical mistrust. No NIMBY/personality, irrationality or cognitive-dissonance labels are implemented.

## Model interpretation and bounded execution

The guided application's `ConversationClient.interpret` performs semantic discovery followed by numeric-free scope review per candidate and **no numeric calls**. It presents score-free meanings, citations, facets, conditions and ambiguity. Users can correct individual meanings or add omissions in their own words. Original testimony (`O.*`), user corrections (`C*.testimony`, `M*.testimony`), choices, clarification text and question background are distinct evidence objects. Background questions and model wording cannot establish citizen evidence. Python checks citation IDs, context and source; exact citizen wording is retrieved for display/export. A targeted correction affects its selected meaning only and retains before/after/provenance. “Unsure” remains available.

Explicit confirmation freezes the interpretation, evidence, version, context and exact proposal with a SHA256 digest. Subsequent edits invalidate confirmation and dependent scores; the initial scored snapshot is never overwritten. Model-proposed wording, citizen-authored testimony and user confirmation are different records. Confirmation checks meaning, not measurement validity. Interpretation schema1.1 binds concern-specific supporting history into the confirmation digest. The numeric reviewer receives the frozen meaning, supporting original passages (including superseded passages after corrections) and current citizen corrections; unrelated original passages are excluded from that concern’s numeric request while the full testimony remains in the session/export. It receives this supporting testimony, and can output only score/rationale; it cannot output changed stance/facets/conditions. Uncertain/ambiguous meanings skip numeric inference. If a clear meaning cannot be supported, the action returns to clarification; no invented score or evidence is substituted.

Each interpretation action has one discovery call reserving1400 output tokens and at most15 semantic scope reviews reserving300 each; at most two attempts per call and32 calls total under180 seconds. The general FN medical-benefit facet nomination occurs before confirmation and assigns no score. Scope-review judgments may still be wrong; unsupported/ambiguous candidates are displayed as needs clarification rather than numerically scored. Each independent numeric concern review reserves300, at most two attempts and at most15 concerns. Each interpretation or profile-scoring stage has180 seconds overall, with120 seconds per HTTP request; final original and conditional profiles are separately bounded stages. Full chat plus reserved output must fit4096, checked with `/tokenize`; evidence is never truncated. Requests are serialized and server concurrency remains1. A final button may invoke two scoring stages (up to360 seconds); the UI live renderer timeout must allow this. Independent stages and repeats add latency. Prompts and reassessment policy are versioned. The old one-shot `LLMClient.__call__` and `legacy_session.py` remain solely for historical synthetic comparison tests/tools; the guided UI uses neither.

Original score anchors, numeric range/null invariants and Python aggregation are unchanged. Numeric review cannot remap a user-confirmed scope; model mistakes may still require explicit clarification. No lexical vetoes, sample recognition, source-score substitution, fixed point bonus, probability/confidence or psychological diagnosis is used. The existing Qwen3-8B revision and V100-only FP16/V0/XFORMERS deployment are unchanged.

## Deterministic aggregation

Assessed scores only enter the unweighted mean. At least three assessed concerns are required for an overall illustrative index; phases need one assessed concern for their mean. Coverage and all missing topics are displayed; these thresholds are experimental choices, not FN coverage rules. A single corridor does not assess all15.

The FN fixture deterministically produces mean4.25, selected privacy minimum2, offset2, cap4, adjusted4 and half-up final4. Live inference never uses this fixture to replace scores.

The generalized bottleneck remains experimental: assessed phase-1 scores<=3 are eligible, select the minimum, add2 and cap the mean. FN p.7 illustrates privacy2+2 but does not establish a universal phase-1 threshold or offset. `aggregate.trace` exports every mean input, denominator, eligible blocker/score/phase, selected minimum, offset, threshold, enabled flag, cap application and rounding. `min(mean,cap)` ensures a cap cannot increase a result. No aggregate is returned below the coverage requirement.

## Confirmation, follow-ups and experimental numerical reassessment

Reference questions/choices remain verbatim from physical pp.8–9. Unsure, optional text, explicit context selection, targeted conflict clarification, joint-proposal question and second confirmation are documented application extensions. Default Q1 requires evidenced original-route opposition/mixed stance and an objection facet of personal_privacy or data_security; perceived physical safety alone is insufficient. The explicit all-reference option does not establish extra assessed topics.

Each presented question has an ID, exact text, source pages and hypothetical flag. Each selected choice, optional clarification and conflict-resolution testimony has a stable ID, question link and context. Joint revisions use unique `J1.*`, `J2.*` IDs and preserve all previous responses/evidence in joint history. Opposite joint choice/text interpretations also require explicit targeted resolution. Selections always refer to that hypothetical. Users choose whether optional text describes the original or modified proposal. Choice and text are interpreted separately. Disagreement on stance/willingness or disappearing facets creates a targeted conflict request; latest wording does not silently override earlier wording. Original-context conflicts with the initial meaning are also explicit. Resolution is user-authored testimony and confirmation is required again. An original concern can be amended or made unsure without restating the entire answer.

Q1 shielding concerns viewing private space, not technical cybersecurity or overall-route acceptance. Q2 bundles altitude/sound/curfew/visibility; residential-route rejection does not independently resolve noise and no single causal effect is claimed. Q3 is a policy preference, not rejection of medical welfare or a diagnosis; its selection alone never changes original welfare scores. Individual-gate testimony is preserved as context history with links, distinct from the required explicit joint answer. The combined proposal spells out every presented Q1/Q2 change plus the unchanged original details. A citizen must accept/reject/be unsure about this exact proposal and state remaining concerns; separate yes answers are insufficient. Q3 is not silently incorporated as a physical modification.

The revised `configs/reassessment.json` policy1.0 is **experimental**; FN supplies illustrative6–7/2–3/cap4 labels, not an implementable numerical update formula. It replaces the previous qualitative-only contract without claiming FN specified this algorithm:

1. **Initial:** score only the versioned confirmed original meaning with the existing2.0 rubric and save an immutable snapshot, evidence, coverage and full aggregate trace.
2. **Final original:** compare confirmed original-context meanings and evidence with the initial confirmed version. Re-review only affected concerns using the same rubric. Carry unaffected numeric/null results unchanged with an explicit reason. Unknown meanings remain null. Questions alone and hypothetical acceptance never retroactively alter an original boundary score. Awareness/benefit/route interpretations are refreshed from their separately confirmed meanings without being new aggregate items.
3. **Conditional:** require explicit joint response and confirmed modified-context meanings. Use the separate acceptance-after-mitigation1.0 anchors: rejection/remaining strong boundaries1–3, substantial remaining reservations4, expressed balanced acceptance5, cautious/qualified acceptance6–7, clear/unqualified acceptance8–9. Uncertainty without a position is null. Scores describe the stated modified proposal, not the original boundary or personal improvement. Only context-supported dimensions enter this profile; absent meanings remain null. Original untested facets make a parent concern unavailable unless a joint clarification explicitly addresses those facets. The exclusion is exported separately; the confirmed meaning is not rewritten. There is no automatic inheritance of medical/equity or other original scores into a modified context.
4. **Aggregation:** recompute each profile independently with the unchanged assessed-only mean, minimum3 concerns, phase summaries, eligible phase-1 minimum+2 cap and half-up rounding. A cap never raises a mean. Answering yes does not remove a cap; only that profile's assessed scores determine eligibility. Coverage changes and missing facets are shown. No new relevant original evidence gives identical initial/final-original scores and aggregate.

Snapshots include raw model integers separately from application arithmetic, per-profile rubric/model/settings, update reasons and evidence. JSON and one-session-per-line JSONL include all15 comparison rows, versions, model-proposed meanings, citizen corrections, confirmations, exact questions/answers, contextual evidence IDs, initial/final/conditional snapshots and traces. Historical exports remain unchanged. Sessions are in memory; explicit exports contain citizen text and routine logs do not.

The reported Q1accept/Q2residential-rejection/Q3unsure pattern still retains multiple simultaneous concerns: privacy viewing is resolved only under shielding; noise is partially tested, routing remains and the policy trade-off is unresolved. No joint acceptance is inferred; original scores are unchanged unless separately clarified. Camera shielding, sound at120m and the extra12-minute time are hypothetical, not engineering/clinical facts. Leading wording and bundled changes remain methodological limitations.

## Universal transitions and conservative facet combination (2026-10-04)

FN physical pp.5–9 provide a scenario, example labels and three illustrative validation outcomes, not a universal numerical update formula. `configs/updates.json`1.0 and typed transition records are application-designed extensions. Neither the original anchors nor assessed-only arithmetic, minimum coverage, phase mapping, bottleneck eligibility/offset or half-up rounding changes here. Blockers are expressed acceptance boundaries and remain separate from numerical cap eligibility.

Each follow-up declares original/hypothetical context, proposal ID, canonical concerns/facets, exact modifications, choice meanings and what cannot be established. Confirmed transitions are unchanged, corrected-original, resolved-under-modification, partially-resolved, remaining-objection, unresolved or untested. Original correction selectively re-reviews affected concerns; confirmation alone and unrelated testimony carry scores. Uncertainty never becomes5. Q1 addresses private-space viewing only; Q2 is a bundled sound/altitude/curfew assumption, with routing/visual choices insufficient to resolve noise; Q3 is policy priority and cannot lower medical support or remove a cap.

The six-question reviewed template bank supports all registered topics: it confirms an unchanged original view or tests an exact citizen-described acceptable change. It supplies no engineering guarantees, costs or feasibility claims. Only actually presented modifications enter the combined proposal. Separate accepted gates never establish joint acceptance. Contradictions require user-authored clarification and updated explicit confirmation. Question assumptions are background, never citizen evidence.

Conditional evidence is reviewed score-free per facet in batches of six within180s; each call reserves1200 output tokens and checks the full4096-token budget without truncation. Required facets are the union of initial and modified interpretations. No original score is silently inherited into a modified proposal. A facet is eligible only with explicit applicability, clear citizen position and contextual evidence; direct modified-context testimony or a relevant resolved/remaining gate plus explicit joint response is required. Every applicable facet receives independent conditional-rubric review. The single concern score is the minimum only if all required facets have numerical reviews; otherwise null. This conservative combination preserves untested safety/security/equity aspects, avoids double counting and may reduce coverage; FN must advise whether a minimum or a different facet-combination rule is appropriate.

Immutable initial, final-original and conditional snapshots have independent aggregates and traces. Transitions export before/after concern scores, independent facet review values, evidence, reasons, rubric and carried/re-reviewed/unavailable status. Blockers retain original status and have separate modified status; one privacy acceptance cannot globally remove them.

Citizen UI has two explicit meaning confirmations combined with subsequent calculation under Continue. Failures preserve confirmed meanings, drafts and answered questions; retry does not duplicate initial snapshots. Numbers appear only at completion. Headline selection is deterministic: available conditional, else available final-original, else insufficient evidence with qualitative positions. Context and modified-assessment unavailability are explicit; changes never imply personal improvement. Schema5.0 separates citizen quotations from explanations and readable summary from audit, retaining a legacy4.1 representation without rewriting historical files.

Questions for FN remain: missing nine-level calibration, ordinal averaging, the4.25/6.0 inconsistency, generalized phase-1 bottleneck, overlapping outcomes and leading/bundled questions; additionally, how to combine facets, establish applicability after modifications, treat remaining boundaries and calibrate conditional scores. Infrastructure and visual candidates receive an independent semantic attribution classifier using only citizen passages and registry-defined contrast categories, without a proposed label: flight routing versus actual facilities/land planning, and viewing privacy versus distinct aesthetic/visible-aircraft reaction. No keyword veto or FN-text recognition is used. A rejected medical nomination cannot reappear through the separate medical-support dimension. Global sufficiency metadata cannot veto scoped evidence.

A broad unsure update cannot overwrite known aspects; targeted clarification is required when an attempted correction ambiguously covers several. Original numerical projection assesses clear supported aspects and retains undecided aspects separately in the confirmed ledger; one concern still enters the aggregate once. Genuine equity opposition/reservations can contribute a distinct meaning, but missing/uncertain equity is never construed as rejection of medical welfare. Modified assessment conservatively requires all genuinely evidenced current aspects; uncertainty still makes a relevant modified parent unavailable.
