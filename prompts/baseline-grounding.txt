For every supported candidate, verify that its exact span_quote appears in its cited answer passage and that the span's meaning supports the claim_type, actor, action, negation and modality. A valid quote alone is insufficient. Questions, role labels, examples, displayed information and correction feedback are not respondent evidence. Never cite these as support. Reject or correct unsupported claims; preserve only claims grounded in the answer.

For each additional claim, use the shortest exact answer span and the same claim schema and taxonomy. Add only substantive meaning clearly present in the answer. Do not infer absences from silence, non-need from a definition, contribution from attendance, actual activity from plans/willingness, or influence from an event merely occurring later. If meaning, actor or activity scope cannot be established, omit the claim or mark a relevant ambiguity; do not guess.

Interpretation distinctions:
- A drone definition is concept knowledge; knowledge that drones carry goods/medical materials or a stated delivery purpose is delivery-use knowledge.
- “Could arrive sooner” is a possible effect without a reason; a reason that explains how/why the effect could occur is reasoned impact. Do not conflate delivery purpose with an established benefit.
- “I attended/listened but did not contribute” supports actual exposure and explicit absence of contribution, not participation.
- A future consultation or intention to comment is hypothetical, not an actual contribution.
- A personal action is not an organisational action; organisational perspective alone does not change the subject.
- Lack of a participation channel or practical access obstacle can be an opportunity barrier; an information request is not by itself an access barrier.
- Unknown outcome stays unknown. “Something changed afterwards” is sequence only unless the respondent links it to their contribution. A reported link is not independently verified causation.

Do not invent experience, local facts, external verification, readiness levels or thematic topic tags. Preserve statements as self-report/belief. For unsupported or unresolved claims, give a short safe reason. Keep different activities, people, times and IAM/transferable domains separate; missing dates alone do not prove contradiction. Keep basic knowledge even when it does not demonstrate impact reasoning. Never select interview questions or add facts from memory.

If correcting a candidate between hypothetical intention and actual activity, the corrected decision must cite a distinct exact answer span that states the actual action or hypothetical plan. Do not turn an intention into actual activity by changing the label while keeping the same supporting span. If no distinct supporting span exists, reject the candidate or leave the interpretation unresolved.
