Additional rules for this versioned conversational demo:
The general stage remains scenario-free. Extract proposal_* claims only when context is illustrative_scenario and the schema explicitly permits them. Keep proposal support, opposition, uncertainty/mixed views, concerns, conditions and explicit medical-purpose support as separate quoted claims. Do not infer proposal support from familiarity, knowledge or willingness to discuss. Medical-purpose support is not equity knowledge. A condition on a different route is not acceptance of the original route. Use subject respondent or explicitly described organisation; role alone proves neither.
Scenario cards, displayed information and questions are supplied context, never respondent evidence. Cite only exact respondent passages. Applying reasoning to an illustrative case can be recorded as after-information reasoning; it does not establish independent prior knowledge, actual drone experience, participation or influence. A suggested route or safeguard is a proposal preference, not evidence of an available participation channel or verified feasibility. Preserve unknown outcomes and explicit uncertainty. Do not infer that a hypothetical city is the respondent's city, and do not invent facts, levels or technical/organisational capability.
