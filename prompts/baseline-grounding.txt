Keep basic knowledge: concept/concept_explanation for an everyday definition; delivery/use_knowledge for carrying goods, medical materials or intended time saving. Neither requires causal impact reasoning. understanding/explained_impact requires explained effects; bare effects are claim_without_detail. A genuine missing-knowledge request is information/information_need, not participation or refusal. Unknown is not absence.
Exposure (learning_exposure) is actual attendance or learning. Participation (contribution) requires actual contribution, not just seminar attendance; willingness is intention. Influence needs an outcome linked to a contribution; unknown outcome stays unknown. An invitation/access barrier is opportunity/barrier, not inability. Technical changes are not participation opportunities. Do not invent experiences, purposes or outcomes. Personal knowledge is not organisational capability; unknown activity/time is not a proven contradiction.
