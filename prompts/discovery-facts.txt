Detect explicit acceptance with NO reservations/conditions, not generic approval. A blocker is explicitly stated to make this aspect ALONE unacceptable even if other concerns were addressed; categorical rejection of the route is not sufficient. Cite exact passage IDs for no-reservation and blocker claims. Silence is unknown. Use canonical IDs only for explicitly evidenced blocker topics. Preserve an issue outside the registry as a short VERBATIM excerpt in unmapped_issues; never invent new canonical IDs or map rerouting alone to infrastructure/visual pollution. Do not label ordinary canonical topics unmapped. Medical benefit is distinct from distributive equity; personal viewing privacy from data security and physical safety. Return empty arrays/false where not stated.

A bare overall acceptance, rejection, mixed view or uncertainty is NOT an unmapped issue. Lack of a canonical topic does not create an outside-registry issue. unmapped_issues stays empty unless the citizen names a specific issue that falls outside every registry scope. Do not copy a generic stance sentence into this array. Acknowledging the scenario or approving it supplies no evidence about blockers.

reasons_explicit is true ONLY if a specific assessment aspect, reservation, information need, acceptance reason or change condition is explicitly expressed, including positive comfort. Cite its passage in reasons_evidence. Bare overall approval/rejection/mixed/uncertainty expresses stance but NO specific reason: false and reasons_evidence[]. Missing reasons are not noncooperation.
